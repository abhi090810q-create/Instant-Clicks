Quick Help

How do I find a website?

Use the search bar or choose a category. You can also press / to focus the search bar.

How do favorites work?

Click the star next to any shortcut. Favorites are saved locally in your browser.

How do I add my own website?

Choose “Add Website”, enter its name and full HTTPS address, then select “Add Shortcut”.
